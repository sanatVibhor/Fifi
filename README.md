# Fifi

### Your orange cat for Data Science.
*She doesn't know everything. She just thinks she does.*

---

Meet **Fifi**.

Fifi is an orange house cat who has taken it upon herself to make sure you actually learn Data Science instead of spending six hours watching videos titled:

> "Machine Learning Roadmap 2026 — Everything You Need To Know"

Fifi does not approve.

She sits on your desk.  
She judges your notes.  
She knocks your confidence off the table.  
And occasionally, she helps you understand Bayes' theorem.

---

## What is Fifi?

**Fifi is a personal Data Science study companion.**

The idea is simple.

Instead of having your learning scattered across:

- ChatGPT conversations
- random bookmarks
- YouTube videos
- PDFs
- Notion pages
- screenshots
- handwritten notes
- "I'll read this later" tabs
- and approximately 47 Chrome tabs you swear are important

Fifi gives everything a home.

Your learning is organized into three main domains:

```text
                 FIFI
                   |
       +-----------+-----------+
       |           |           |
       v           v           v
   Statistics    Machine      RAG &
   & Maths       Learning     GenAI
```

Each domain contains topics.

Each topic contains concepts.

Each concept can contain notes, resources, questions and conversations.

Eventually, Fifi will know all of it.

# How Fifi Works

Every topic gets its own little home.

For example:

```text
Statistics & Mathematics
|
+-- Probability
|   +-- Sample Space
|   +-- Events
|   +-- Conditional Probability
|   +-- Independence
|   +-- Bayes Theorem
|
+-- Distributions
|   +-- Normal
|   +-- Binomial
|   +-- Poisson
|   +-- Exponential
|
+-- Hypothesis Testing
    +-- Null Hypothesis
    +-- P-value
    +-- Type I Error
    +-- Type II Error
```

Each topic can contain different types of knowledge.

### Notes

Your own understanding.

Not textbook definitions copied into a box.

The goal is:

> "Can I explain this without looking at the formula?"

---

### Resources

Add anything useful:

- ChatGPT conversations
- Articles
- YouTube videos
- Research papers
- Documentation
- Courses
- Books

Because apparently one browser bookmark folder wasn't enough.

---

### Questions

Keep track of things that made you think:

> "Wait... why?"

For example:

**Question**

Why does zero covariance not imply independence?

**Status**

Needs Revision

**Eventually**

Understood.

Fifi is proud.

# Progress

Fifi keeps track of where you actually are.

Not where you *feel* you are.

Topics can have four states:

```text
Not Started

In Progress

Completed

Needs Revision
```

Because completing a topic once doesn't mean your brain has permanently installed it.

Unfortunately.

# Fifi V2 — The Cat Gets Smarter

Version 1 is intentionally simple.

Fifi is currently just a very organized cat.

But in **V2**, she gets a brain.

The plan is to run a small local LLM and give Fifi access to the entire study system.

Something along the lines of:

```text
                 FIFI
                   |
                   v
                Query
                   |
                   v
            Retrieval Layer
                   |
       +-----------+-----------+
       |           |           |
       v           v           v
     Notes       Topics      Resources
       |           |           |
       +-----------+-----------+
                   |
                   v
                  RAG
                   |
                   v
              Local LLM
                   |
                   v
              Fifi's Answer
```

So instead of asking a generic AI:

> "Explain bias variance."

You could ask Fifi:

> "Explain bias variance based on what I've already studied, connect it to linear regression, and tell me what I'm still weak at."

And Fifi can use your actual learning context.

# Why Fifi?

Because every serious project needs an unnecessarily confident orange cat.

But there's a more serious reason.

**Fifi is not supposed to replace learning.**

She's supposed to make learning easier to organize, revisit and connect.

The human still has to do the hard part.

Fifi can explain gradient descent fourteen different ways.

She cannot make you remember it.

She's a cat.

# The Philosophy

Fifi follows one simple rule:

> **Don't just collect knowledge. Build understanding.**

The goal isn't to have 500 bookmarked resources.

It's to eventually be able to:

**Understand → Explain → Apply → Connect → Defend**

a concept.

Because in an interview, nobody cares that you once watched a 47-minute video about logistic regression.

They care whether you can explain:

- why it works
- when it fails
- what assumptions it makes
- how it compares to alternatives
- and what you would do differently
